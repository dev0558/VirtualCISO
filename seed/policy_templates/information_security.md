# Information Security Policy

**Organisation:** {{ company_name }}
**Classification:** Internal
**Owner:** {{ security_owner }}
**Review cadence:** At least annually

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy sets out {{ company_name }}'s commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of customer data, employee data, intellectual property and the systems that process them. It applies to all employees, contractors and third parties with access to {{ company_name }} information, and to all systems including {{ systems }}.

## 2. Roles and responsibilities
- **Executive sponsor ({{ executive_sponsor }}):** accountable for information security risk and for approving this policy.
- **Security owner ({{ security_owner }}):** maintains the information security programme, risk register and policy set; reports posture to leadership quarterly.
- **System owners:** implement and operate controls for the systems they own.
- **All personnel:** follow this policy and topic-specific policies and report suspected incidents immediately.

## 3. Security objectives
1. Restrict access to information to authorised people with a business need (least privilege).
2. Require multi-factor authentication for all user and administrative access.
3. Protect {{ data_types }} in transit and at rest using current, approved encryption.
4. Detect, respond to and recover from incidents in a timely, documented way.
5. Continuously monitor technical controls against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A and NIST CSF 2.0.

## 4. Topic-specific policies
This policy is supported by the Access Control Policy, Incident Response Plan, Backup and Recovery Policy and Acceptable Use Policy. Documented operating procedures are maintained for critical systems.

## 5. Compliance and exceptions
Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-assessed and approved by the security owner with a defined expiry. Violations may lead to disciplinary action.

## 6. Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually and after significant organisational or technical change.
